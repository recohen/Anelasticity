Generated by `tools/anelastic_collect.py`. Every point is also in `results_all.csv`.

Sweep moduli include the one-step lag correction $M^*_{\rm corr}=M^*_{\rm lock-in}\,e^{i\omega\Delta t}$, $\Delta t=1$ fs (Eq. 10; lockin_results.txt files are uncorrected).

Moduli from the lock-in fit (sweep) or Green–Kubo (GK, NVE). Shear: $G^*$. Uniaxial x: $M^*=K^*+\frac43G^*$.

$$G''(\omega)=\eta_0\,\omega+O(\omega^3)\;\Rightarrow\;\eta_0=\lim_{\omega\to0}\frac{dG''}{d\omega} \tag{1}$$
$$M''(\omega)=(\zeta_0+\tfrac43\eta_0)\,\omega+O(\omega^3) \tag{2}$$
$$\left.\frac{dG''}{d\omega}\right|_{\bar\omega_{i}}\approx\frac{G''_{i+1}-G''_i}{\omega_{i+1}-\omega_i},\quad \bar\omega_i=\sqrt{\omega_i\omega_{i+1}} \tag{3}$$
$$\eta_0=\frac{\sum_i w_i\omega_iG''_i}{\sum_i w_i\omega_i^2},\quad w_i=\sigma_i^{-2},\ f_i\le200\ \mathrm{GHz} \tag{4}$$
$$\frac{dG''}{d\omega}=\int_0^{t_c}G(t)\left[\cos\omega t-\omega t\sin\omega t\right]dt\ \text{(GK)} \tag{5}$$
$$\eta'(\omega)=G''(\omega)/\omega \tag{6}$$
$$Q^{-1}=\tan\delta=\frac{G''}{G'}\ \text{or}\ \frac{M''}{M'},\qquad Q=\frac{1}{\tan\delta} \tag{8}$$
$$\sigma_{Q^{-1}}^2=\left(\frac{\sigma_{G''}}{G'}\right)^2+\left(\frac{G''\sigma_{G'}}{G'^2}\right)^2,\qquad \sigma_Q=\frac{\sigma_{Q^{-1}}}{Q^{-2}} \tag{9}$$
For $|G''|>|G'|$ (liquid-like, $Q<1$) $Q$ and $\sigma_Q$ are computed directly from $Q=G'/G''$. Unresolved points get 2σ bounds: $Q>1/(\max(Q^{-1},0)+2\sigma_{Q^{-1}})$ when the loss is unresolved, $Q<\max(Q,0)+2\sigma_Q$ when the storage modulus is unresolved.
