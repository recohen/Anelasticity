# Temperature dependence: single activation energy or not?

Generated by `tools/T_dependence.py` from `results_gk.csv` and `results_all.csv`. Every series is at fixed V, so p rises with T and the energies are isochoric.

$$E_{loc}=s\,k_B\,\frac{\Delta\ln y}{\Delta(1/T)}\quad(s=+1:\ \eta,\zeta;\ s=-1:\ D,\ Q^{-1}) \tag{1}$$
$$\ln y=a+s\,\frac{E}{k_BT} \tag{2}$$
$$\ln y=a+n\ln T \tag{3}$$
$$\ln y=a+s\,\frac{B}{T-T_0} \tag{4}$$

A single activation energy means a constant $E_{loc}$ and $\chi^2/\nu\approx1$ for Eq. 2. A T-independent quantity has $n\approx0$ in Eq. 3. With two points only $E_{loc}$ is defined. Units: η, ζ in mPa s; D in $10^{-9}$ m²/s.
