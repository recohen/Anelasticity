# MgO anelasticity and viscosity from oscillatory-shear NEMD

Started 2026-10-05. System: MgO, 4096 atoms, $V = 6.6868$ Å$^3$/atom (constant volume, about 190 GPa at 8000 K), DeePMD potential `graph.pb` (Mg, O; $r_c = 7$ Å), LAMMPS `~/bin/lmp` (22Jul2025) with conda env `deepmd_py311`, Tesla P100.

## 1. Quantities

Imposed shear strain and measured stress (sign: $\sigma_{xz} = -P_{xz}$):

$$\gamma(t) = \gamma_0 \sin\omega t, \qquad \gamma = \mathrm{xz}/L_z \tag{1}$$

$$\sigma(t) = \gamma_0\,[\,G'(\omega)\sin\omega t + G''(\omega)\cos\omega t\,] + \text{noise} \tag{2}$$

$$G^*(\omega) = G' + iG'', \qquad \tan\delta = G''/G' = Q^{-1}, \qquad \eta'(\omega) = G''/\omega \tag{3}$$

In Gross's framework the complex modulus is generated by a relaxation spectrum $H(\tau)$:

$$G^*(\omega) = G_e + \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} H(\tau)\,\frac{i\omega\tau}{1+i\omega\tau}\,d\ln\tau \tag{4}$$

with relaxation function $G(t) = G_e + \int H(\tau)e^{-t/\tau}\,d\ln\tau$ and $G^*(\omega) = i\omega\int_0^\infty G(t)e^{-i\omega t}dt$. Green–Kubo gives $G(t) = (V/k_BT)\langle\sigma_{xz}(0)\sigma_{xz}(t)\rangle$ directly, so it is the equilibrium cross-check of Eq. (4).

A uniaxial $x$ wiggle (run R1A1) changes volume and measures the longitudinal modulus:

$$M^* = K^* + \tfrac{4}{3}G^*, \qquad M''/\omega = \zeta + \tfrac{4}{3}\eta \tag{5}$$

## 2. Analysis: lock-in at the known drive frequency

`~/Claude/tools/anelastic_lockin.py` fits stress and strain to $a + bt + \sum_{k=1}^{3}[s_k\sin k\omega t + c_k\cos k\omega t]$ and forms $G^* = \hat\sigma_1/\hat\gamma_1$. Error bars come from whole-cycle blocks. Harmonics $|\hat\sigma_2/\hat\sigma_1|$, $|\hat\sigma_3/\hat\sigma_1|$ flag nonlinearity. Stresses written as $N_{av}$-step trailing averages are corrected exactly by

$$H(\omega) = \frac{\sin(N_{av}\omega\Delta t/2)}{N_{av}\sin(\omega\Delta t/2)}\,e^{-i\omega(N_{av}-1)\Delta t/2} \tag{6}$$

Box flips at $|\mathrm{xz}| > L_x/2$ are unwrapped, so strain amplitudes above 0.5 are allowed.

Reanalysis of the existing 8000 K runs (1 THz):

| run | $\gamma_0$ | modulus | $\delta$ | loss$/\omega$ (Pa s) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 54671682, xz shear | 0.0066 | $G'=15.5\pm3.6$, $G''=42.9\pm4.9$ GPa | 70° | $\eta' = 0.0068\pm0.0008$ |
| R1A1, x uniaxial | 0.033 | $M'=537.5\pm1.6$, $M''=89.1\pm0.9$ GPa | 9.4° | $\zeta+\frac43\eta = 0.0142\pm0.0001$ |

A Maxwell fit to the shear point gives $G_\infty \approx 135$ GPa, $\tau \approx 0.057$ ps, $\eta_0 \approx 7.7$ mPa s.

## 3. Noise and run length

Fluctuation–dissipation sets the lock-in noise on the stress amplitude:

$$\delta\sigma^2 \simeq C\,\frac{k_BT\,G''(\omega)}{\omega V T_{run}} \tag{7}$$

$C \approx 8$ reproduces the measured error of run 54671682. The relative error on $G''$ is then

$$\epsilon = \frac{1}{\gamma_0}\sqrt{\frac{C k_BT}{\omega G'' V T_{run}}} \;\xrightarrow{\;\omega\tau\ll1\;}\; \frac{1}{\dot\gamma_0}\sqrt{\frac{C k_BT}{\eta V T_{run}}}, \qquad \dot\gamma_0=\omega\gamma_0 \tag{8}$$

In the viscous regime the error depends on the strain-rate amplitude $\dot\gamma_0$, not on frequency. The same noise limits Green–Kubo at low frequency, but NEMD can raise the signal by raising $\gamma_0$. Amplitude rule:

$$\gamma_0 = \mathrm{clip}(\dot\gamma_0/\omega,\ \gamma_{min},\ \gamma_{max}), \quad \dot\gamma_0 = 10^{11}\,\mathrm{s^{-1}},\ \gamma_{min}=0.01,\ \gamma_{max}=5 \tag{9}$$

The Weissenberg number $\dot\gamma_0\tau \approx 6\times10^{-3}$ at 8000 K, so the liquid should be linear even at large strain. This must be checked with two amplitudes at one frequency.

## 4. Frequency sweep

`~/Claude/tools/anelastic_sweep_setup.py` writes task directories, `tasks.txt` and a SLURM array `sweep.sh` (user's P100 header). `anelastic_sweep.lammps` equilibrates 20 ps NVT, then runs xz wiggle + `nvt/sllod` in whole-period chunks with a restart after each chunk. Default sweep at 8000 K: periods 0.2, 1, 5, 20, 100 ps (5 THz to 10 GHz, 2.7 decades), 10% target error on $G''$, at least 3 cycles and 20 ps per point. Cost at the old uncompressed P100 speed (0.115 s/step) is about 0.6 M steps, 0.8 P100-days in total, under a day of wall time as an array. A linearity check repeats the 20 ps point at $\dot\gamma_0 = 3\times10^{10}$ s$^{-1}$.

The cost floor is set by cycles, not noise: one period at frequency $f$ costs $1/(f\,\Delta t)$ steps, i.e. $10^6$ steps (about 1.3 P100-days) per period at 1 GHz. Frequencies below 10 GHz are therefore only run where they carry information, i.e. at lower $T$ where $\tau$ has grown.

## 5. Open points

- Timing of the compressed model (`dp compress`) on P100 sets the real cost.
- At 8000 K, $\omega\tau \ll 1$ below about 100 GHz, so the liquid is Newtonian there. The informative low-frequency structure appears on cooling, as $\tau$ grows into the window.
- Cooling at constant volume until crystallization. Shear may promote crystallization in the supercooled liquid, so $\gamma_{max}$ will need to come down there.
